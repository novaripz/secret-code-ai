Here's the whole thing. Six steps, about 20 minutes, most of it just waiting on
downloads. You quit the game once, at step 2. That's it.

You need Minecraft for Windows (Bedrock Edition) - the one you already play with
your friends.


THE STEPS
---------

1. Download the pack and unzip it:
   https://github.com/novaripz/secret-code-ai/archive/refs/heads/claude/bedrock-streamer-pack-bs2c1j.zip

   Right-click the ZIP > Extract All. Open the "streamer-minecraft" folder inside.
   Keep it on your Desktop.

2. Open Minecraft and make the world you want to play in.
   In the world settings turn on "Multiplayer Game" and "Require players to
   accept resource packs".
   Then quit Minecraft completely.

3. Double-click START-HERE.cmd
   It opens all the download pages in your browser.

4. Click the green Download button on every page.
   Leave the files in your Downloads folder. Don't rename them, don't unzip them,
   don't move them.

5. Go back to the setup window and press Enter.
   It installs everything, switches it all on in your world, and opens Minecraft
   for you.

6. In Minecraft: Settings > Video > Graphics Mode > Vibrant Visuals.
   Open your world. Play.

Your friends join from Play > Friends while your world is open and tap accept on
the download prompt. They install nothing.

Achievements are off in this world - one of the addons needs a world setting that
switches them off. Everything else works normally.


THE FOUR BUTTONS YOU'LL USE
---------------------------

  3-BEAUTIFUL-MODE.cmd    full atmosphere
  4-PERFORMANCE-MODE.cmd  same addons, better FPS
  11-OPTIMIZE-STREAM.cmd  tunes the game for a steady 60 while live
  5-BACKUP-WORLD.cmd      saves a copy of your world right now

Close Minecraft before running any of them, open it again after. Each one takes a
backup first, so nothing is permanent.

IF SOMETHING LOOKS OFF
----------------------
Double-click 1-CHECK-SETUP.cmd - it tells you what's installed and what's on.
It's almost always one of these three:

  Minecraft was open while you ran a script  ->  close it and run the script again
  You picked the wrong world from the list   ->  run it again, pick the right one
  No aurora in the sky                       ->  Settings > Video > Graphics Mode
